Role · U.S. Coast Guard / Harbor patrol
Boarding inspection in seconds, not minutes
Law enforcement needs to verify registration, insurance, and owner identity quickly during a boarding. Today that means paper documents, often wet, often expired, sometimes missing. The Moxie QR replaces all of that.
Registration number + expiry
Auth required
The #1 thing they check. State registration is required by law on all recreational vessels. A Moxie QR that shows current registration on scan replaces the paper card entirely — and can't be lost or water-damaged.
Insurance verification
Auth required
Increasingly required. While federal law doesn't mandate insurance, California marinas and many waterways are moving toward requiring it. Having it instantly verifiable adds legitimacy.
Owner name + emergency contact
Auth required
Critical in a rescue scenario. If a vessel is found adrift or abandoned, identifying and contacting the owner fast can save lives. Scanning the stern QR gives responders everything they need immediately.
Hull ID (HIN)
Auth required
Theft and fraud prevention. Cross-referencing the HIN against the profile confirms the vessel is who it says it is. Stolen vessels often have altered documentation — a verified digital record is harder to fake.
Access method
Special code
Phase 2 feature: emergency access code. A unique one-time or time-limited code printed on the sticker (separate from the QR) lets authorized personnel access private fields without a full account login. Simple and practical.
The Coast Guard's scan experience
Boarding officer scans the QR on the stern. Registration number, expiry date, and insurance details load immediately. No wet paper, no "I left it at home." Boarding takes 90 seconds instead of 10 minutes. This is a pitch you can literally make to the USCIS Auxiliary — they would love to endorse this.
