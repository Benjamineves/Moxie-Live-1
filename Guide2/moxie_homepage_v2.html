How it works

Three steps.
Always working.

01

We create your vessel profile

Make, model, year, HIN, home marina, insurance, registration — everything in one persistent digital identity. You control what's shared and with whom.

02

Sticker goes on the boat

A premium weatherproof vinyl QR sticker — 3M marine-grade with gloss overlaminate. Designed to last, built to look right on a yacht.

03

Anyone can scan it

A marina operator sees emergency contacts. The Coast Guard sees registration. The public sees vessel specs. The owner sees everything — and can edit.

Who it's for

One scan.
Four audiences.

Boat Owner

Your boat's command center

Full read/write access to every field. Insurance docs, registration, maintenance logs — always with the boat, always current.

  • Store insurance, registration, boater card
  • Manage who sees what
  • Edit from any phone — no app needed
Marina Operator

Every slip, verified

Emergency contacts, insurance verification, and vessel data — accessible by scanning the QR on the dock box or hull.

  • Emergency contact access in seconds
  • Insurance verification on demand
  • Slip occupancy management
General Public

Admirers welcome

Vessel specs — make, model, year, type. Nothing private. The digital equivalent of reading the name on the transom.

  • Make, model, year, vessel type
  • No personal data exposed
  • Clean, beautiful profile page
Coast Guard

Boarding inspections in seconds

Registration number, expiry, HIN, and insurance — everything needed for a boarding inspection, instantly.

  • Registration & HIN verification
  • Insurance status & expiry
  • Emergency & safety data
